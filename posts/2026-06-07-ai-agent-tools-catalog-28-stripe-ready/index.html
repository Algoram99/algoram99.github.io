What's missing from the catalog

Three categories of work the Algoram99 storefront deliberately does not sell, because the binding constraint is delivery, not a missing product page:

  • Subscriptions and SaaS. Every product is a one-time purchase (with the single exception of the 6-month intelligence subscription). There is no monthly retainer, no per-seat license, no annual fee. This is a deliberate constraint — recurring billing has higher support cost per dollar than one-shot artifact delivery, and the catalog is optimized for the latter.
  • Coaching or implementation services. If you want help adopting the PAI v5.0.0 guide or any of the security CLIs, the product is the artifact. The catalog does not sell hours, only deliverables.
  • Free tools. All 40 products have a price. There is no "free tier" and no "community edition." The lower bound of the catalog is $9, not $0, because the maintenance cost of supporting a free tier alongside paid products is not sustainable at this scale.
